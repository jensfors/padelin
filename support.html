Common Requests

Profile setup

  • Use the search bar to find your Rankedin profile.
  • If the profile is missing, confirm the name and spelling in Rankedin.

Bookmarks and preferences

  • Bookmarked clubs and events are stored locally on your device.
  • Clearing app storage removes bookmarks and saved filters.

Search results

  • Results come directly from Rankedin, so availability depends on their data.
  • Try adjusting filters or search terms if you do not see what you expect.
